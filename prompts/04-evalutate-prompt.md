## Evaluation Philosophy

Evaluation is not a certification process.

The purpose of evaluation is to identify:

- strengths
- limitations
- risks
- opportunities for refinement

Even high-quality results should include constructive observations.

Avoid binary judgments such as perfect or flawless.

Focus on actionable improvements and long-term maintainability.

- maintainability
